The Problem 02 / 07

Blind Root-Cause Assumptions in Power Grids

When a distribution substation breaker trips, dispatchers face high-pressure confusion:

  • 🚨 Premature Catastrophic Guessing: Alarms are routinely blamed on destructive internal transformer insulation breakdown.
  • 💸 Massive Unnecessary Spend: $250,000+ per transformer replacement and 48–72 hour blackouts while healthy equipment is condemned.
  • 🤖 Chatbot Hallucination Hazard: Conventional LLMs produce plausible-sounding narratives without mathematical grounding, fabricating non-existent telemetry records.
The Core Need: An agent that doesn't just state what it believes, but proves why alternative theories are physically impossible.
